Meet the Founder

Army veteran, former firefighter / paramedic, father, and founder of Veteran Detail Services. Built on discipline, service, cleanliness, and trust.

Meet Jake

My name is Jake, founder of Veteran Detail Services and a proud resident of Southwest Florida. I served on active duty in the United States Army from 2007 to 2014 as a Forward Observer, including deployment to the Middle East. I was medically retired following injuries sustained while deployed.

After the military, I continued serving my community as a firefighter and paramedic in Lee County. Those experiences shaped the discipline, urgency, attention to detail, and service mindset that now guide every part of this business.

Veteran Detail Services was built to provide professional vehicle care with a focus on quality, cleanliness, and trust. We believe in honest service, premium-but-fair pricing, and results that make a real difference in how a vehicle looks and feels.

OUR WHY

Why This Business Exists

Our son Bentley was born at 22 weeks and remains medically fragile. That changed how our family views cleanliness, especially in the vehicle we rely on daily. This business was built from that experience — to deliver not only a better-looking vehicle, but a cleaner one too.

Our Mission

Veteran Detail Services combines professional detailing with a service-driven, health-conscious approach to vehicle care. We want every customer to feel they received real value, clear communication, and a vehicle they can take pride in.
